Cubefall support

Cubefall is a cube puzzle game for iPhone. Turn the cube, twist its layers, and race the clock in Rush or make solid sides in Solve.

Contact

Questions, bugs, or ideas: cannonevans.biz@gmail.com. You can also tap Feedback on the start screen or after a game.

Common questions

  • How do I twist the cube? Swipe across a tile. Drag on empty space to turn the whole cube.
  • What do hints do? A hint wiggles the layer to twist next and shows which way.
  • What are hourglasses? In Rush, the clock pauses at 5 seconds and offers more time. An hourglass adds 15 seconds.
  • A purchase didn't arrive. Hints and hourglasses are consumable items. Close and reopen the app so the App Store can redeliver it. If it still hasn't arrived, email us with the date of the purchase.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Daily rewards can be claimed once per day. Missing a day starts the 7-day calendar over.

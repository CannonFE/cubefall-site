CUBEFALL

Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Cubefall is made by Cannon Evans ("we"). This policy explains what the Cubefall iPhone app collects and why. Cubefall has no accounts and we never ask for your name, email, or phone number in the app.

What we collect

  • Gameplay analytics. To understand how the game is played and to tune its difficulty, the app sends events such as games started and finished, scores, hints used, continues taken, ads shown, and daily rewards claimed. Each install gets a random identifier; it is not linked to your identity. Events also include basic device details (model, iOS version, app version, language, and time zone). We use PostHog (United States) to process this data. Like any internet service, PostHog receives your IP address and may use it to estimate an approximate location such as country or city.
  • Purchases. Hints and hourglasses are bought through Apple. We receive which item was bought; we never receive your payment details.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback in the app, we receive your star rating and any comment you type. Please don't include personal details in comments.
  • Game Center. If you sign in to Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple's leaderboards. Your Game Center name and picture are shown in the app but are not sent to us.

Ads

Cubefall shows ads from Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch for extra time or moves, and occasional ads between games. Google may collect device identifiers, IP address, and ad interactions to serve and measure ads. The advertising identifier is only used if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt; you can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK) the app asks for consent first, and a Privacy choices button on the start screen lets you change it. Ads are limited to general-audience content. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Stored on your device

Your best scores, hint and hourglass counts, daily reward progress, and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them and resets the random analytics identifier.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't use your data to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Google may, only if you allow tracking.
  • The game's difficulty is never changed based on ads or on how much you might spend.

How long we keep data

Analytics and feedback are kept for up to one year, then deleted.

Children

Cubefall is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off ad tracking in iOS Settings, change ad consent with Privacy choices, or delete the app to remove local data and reset the analytics identifier. To ask about or delete data we hold, email us; because the app has no accounts, include roughly when you played or sent feedback so we can find it. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law) you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data, and we will honor them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
